Mermaid with Pele

Pele renders Mermaid to SVG; the board camera transforms an SVG group around it, so the diagram stays sharp at any zoom.

  • Ctrl + Scroll to zoom, Middle Mouse Drag or Space + Left Drag to pan
  • Trackpad: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
  • Touch: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
