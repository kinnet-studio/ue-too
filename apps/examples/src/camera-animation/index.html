Camera Animation

Smooth camera flights with createZoomPanPath from @ue-too/board, driven by @ue-too/animate. On a long trip the camera zooms out, glides across, and zooms back in.

  • Click anywhere to fly the camera there
  • Back to start flies the camera back to where it began, marked with an orange cross
  • Pan or zoom during a flight to take over the camera
  • Scroll to zoom, Middle Mouse Drag or Space + Left Drag to pan manually
  • Trackpad: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
  • Touch: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
  • A ruler overlay shows the current viewport boundaries
