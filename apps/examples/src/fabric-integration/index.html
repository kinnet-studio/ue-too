Fabric.js Integration

Integrates uē-tôo camera controls with Fabric.js. Toggle between movement mode (pan/zoom the viewport) and selection mode (select and manipulate Fabric objects).

  • In Movement mode: Scroll to zoom, Middle Mouse Drag or Space + Left Drag to pan
  • Trackpad: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
  • Touch: Two-finger drag to pan, Pinch to zoom
  • In Selection mode: click to select Fabric objects ("Hello world!" text and rectangle)
  • Click the button below to switch modes
